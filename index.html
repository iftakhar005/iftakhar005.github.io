QMedLeafNet

Parameter-efficient hybrid quantum–classical network for medicinal plant health detection

A parameter-matched study of medicinal plant disease classification. Frozen MobileNetV3-Small features feed four classical heads deliberately matched in trainable parameters, establishing the baseline against which the quantum head is evaluated. Accepted as an oral paper at IEEE ICCIT 2026.

Contribution: Research lead — model design, data pipeline and evaluation.

PyTorchTensorFlowscikit-learnPython
Repository
Technical highlights
  • MobileNetV3-Small backbone — 927K parameters, frozen for feature extraction
  • Four parameter-matched heads: logistic regression, SVM-RBF, SVM-linear and MLP
  • 25,962 indexed images (1,981 originals + 23,981 augmented)
  • 7 species × 9 conditions = 63 classes; 5-fold splits with no parent leakage

Protein Thermostability Mining

ESM-2 protein language models

Investigating whether pretrained protein representations predict thermostability from sequence — and whether task-specific fine-tuning actually helps.

  • ESM-2 8M mean-pooled embeddings with chunking for long sequences
  • 6,722 sequences: 5,310 train / 706 validation / 706 test
  • Best: frozen ESM-2 + MLP — MAE 0.1385, RMSE 0.1699, R² 0.3560
  • Fine-tuning the final transformer layer did not improve test generalisation

MechanismSelector

Energy-efficient concept drift adaptation

A cost-ordered try-and-escalate policy: attempt a cheap incremental nudge, verify it on a held-out suffix, and rebuild only if it fails. The primary metric is deterministic work_units; energy is logged only as corroboration.

Contribution: Research design, implementation and statistical analysis.

Pythonscikit-learnXGBoostRiverCodeCarbon
Repository
Technical highlights
  • Five policies across five streams, four model families and five seeds — 500 prequential runs
  • Cheaper than AlwaysRebuild in 51/60 blocks with no detectable accuracy difference between policies
  • Friedman/Nemenyi and Holm-corrected paired tests with determinism and sensitivity checks
